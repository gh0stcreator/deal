# Safety and Privacy Rules

1. Raw private statements from one side are never shown to the other side.
2. Synthesis must be neutral and de-escalating.
3. Manipulative, abusive, or toxic language must be transformed into safe neutral formulations.
4. The bot must not claim legal enforceability.
5. Consent is required before any synthesized reuse.
6. Access is role-based by session participant identity.
7. State transitions must gate what data can be processed and displayed.
